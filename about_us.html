About the business

Our goal as the Royal Chaffeurs is to ensure every customer feels safe, comfortable and reliant on us

Royal Chaffeurs is a small business that helps people travel in comfort. We offer various services such as collecting people from the airport and taking them to their hotels, meetings as well as conferences.

We use luxury cars such as BMWs, Mercedes benz and Hyuandi because our customers deserve to travel like royalty, in style as well as arrive in style.

. We aim to ensure that our customer to travel and enjoy their trip no matter how short it is without having to worry about HOW they will get to their destination.

Our customers are tourists, business people, company managers, government officials and other important guests who come to Johannesburg. Hotels and companies are welcome to book our services for their visitors.
